GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before
you go?

A few helpful answers to get you started.

Can I book a taxi in advance?+

This prototype is designed for advance booking requests. Final booking windows and confirmation rules should be set by LIA's dispatch team.

Do you offer airport transfers to YUL?+

Airport transfers are planned as a core LIA service. Confirm availability, pickup instructions and the fare with the company before launch.

Can I request a larger vehicle?+

Yes, the proposed booking flow includes a minivan option. Vehicle availability and passenger or luggage limits must be confirmed with dispatch.

Where does LIA Taxi operate?+

The prototype highlights Montreal, Laval, Longueuil, the North Shore, South Shore and YUL as proposed target areas. The final list should reflect actual operating coverage.
